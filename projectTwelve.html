Image Templates

2026

Product photography without the shoot. This is a library of 300+ image templates I designed for AI generation. Each one is a locked, hand-tuned prompt with swappable reference slots: the product, the person, the outfit and the scene all come in as images, and the template holds the art direction while they swap.

The multi-reference system was the part I had to build. One slot makes a product shot; four make a full campaign image with a cast. Hover any preview for the references that went in; click to open it up with the prompt. Generated with ChatGPT Image 2 and Nano Banana Pro.
