Cultural Power Index

2025

In 2025, M+C Saatchi rebuilt itself around a single idea: Cultural Power. The belief that brands that shape culture outperform the ones that just show up in it. To back the idea with proof, the group built the Cultural Power Index, an AI-powered tool that measures the cultural power of thousands of brands in real time.

A big claim needs a big entrance. I designed and animated the hype reel that launched the index, giving a data tool the pace and punch of a title sequence.
