Solflare

2025

Solflare is one of the biggest wallets on Solana, with over three million people using it every month. Ragged Edge rebranded it as the Stronghold of the Free: gothic type borrowed from centuries of banking history, set against a yellow you can't ignore. A crypto brand built on trust rather than hype.

Working with the team at Ragged Edge, I brought the brand to life in motion, animating the launch films: the manifesto, product sizzles, explainers and the memes. The launch pulled in 44 million impressions, over 3,200 memes and at least one tattoo.
